01 / ABOUT

Developer, maker & avid gardener.

I enjoy building things - whether that's software, something useful for my allotment, or a project made by hand.

I'm a software developer with a curiosity for understanding how things work and a habit of turning that curiosity into projects. Away from the screen, you'll usually find me at my allotment, experimenting in the garden or working on something for my family.

Gardening has taught me to be patient, learn from mistakes and appreciate that good things take time. I enjoy bringing that same mindset to software: breaking problems down, learning what I don't know and gradually turning an idea into something that works.

When I'm not coding or gardening, I enjoy sewing, reading and gaming. I'm particularly drawn to projects where I can learn a completely new skill along the way.
